How to Play Sur Kharvaa

Master the traditional sightless Mongolian composite bow in three easy steps.

Step 1

Pull Back to Draw

Click or tap anywhere on the field and drag downward to bend the composite bow and build power.

Step 2

Aim Low & Across

Move left or right to line up with a leather sur. Adjust how far down you pull to raise or flatten your arc!

Step 3

Read the Khadag Flags

No sights allowed! Watch the colorful silk prayer flags fluttering in the steppe wind and aim slightly into the breeze.

Keyboard option: Use Left/Right arrows to aim, Up/Down to adjust power & arc, and hold/release Spacebar to shoot.
